# Awesome iOS Apps for Students

A curated list of iPhone and iPad apps that help students study, stay focused, prepare for exams and jobs, and manage money — with an emphasis on **pay-once, ad-free, on-device** tools that suit a student budget. Mixes well-known apps with smaller indie ones.

> Students get hit hardest by subscriptions and ads. Where an app is a one-time purchase or works offline/on-device, it's noted.

> **Disclosure:** This list is maintained by Lumi Studio, developer of the Lumi Studio apps named below. Entries are factual and non-ranked; other developers' apps are included for context.

## Note-taking & Scanning

- [Notability](https://apps.apple.com/app/id360593530) - Popular note-taking app with a one-time purchase option.
- [ScanTo Pro](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6779977651) - Scan lecture notes and handouts to searchable PDFs with OCR; on-device, pay-once. · [in-depth guide](https://alice51849.github.io/ios-app-guide/hubs/scanto.html)
- [Sono Note](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6782139553) - Record lectures and get an on-device transcript, summary, and key points. · [in-depth guide](https://alice51849.github.io/ios-app-guide/hubs/sononote.html)
- [100 Notes Studio](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6798813048) - Lumi Studio offline notebook for handwriting, typed notes, PDF markup and page-attached audio, built around the iPad. · [in-depth guide](https://alice51849.github.io/ios-app-guide/hubs/notesstudio100.html)

## Writing & Presentations

- [OnePage PPT](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6798814385) - Lumi Studio tool that condenses a wall of text into a single summary slide. · [in-depth guide](https://alice51849.github.io/ios-app-guide/hubs/onepageppt.html)
- [AI Brief](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6791658210) - Lumi Studio tool that gathers screenshots, PDFs and notes into one organized brief before you hand context to an AI assistant. · [in-depth guide](https://alice51849.github.io/ios-app-guide/hubs/aibriefpack.html)
- [SaveTag](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6802505528) - Lumi Studio read-later manager for saving links now and actually getting back to them later. · [in-depth guide](https://alice51849.github.io/ios-app-guide/hubs/savetag.html)

## Focus & Productivity

- [Forest](https://apps.apple.com/app/id866450515) - Focus timer that gamifies staying off your phone.
- [LockHour Pro](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6780107485) - Hard-block distracting apps for timed study sessions via Apple Screen Time; pay-once. · [in-depth guide](https://alice51849.github.io/ios-app-guide/hubs/lockhour.html)
- [Mochi](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6785004775) - Cute, simple checklist for assignments and deadlines; free base, one-time unlock. · [in-depth guide](https://alice51849.github.io/ios-app-guide/hubs/mochi.html)

## Exams & Career

- [Anki (AnkiMobile)](https://apps.apple.com/app/id373493387) - Spaced-repetition flashcards; one-time purchase on iOS.
- [Aim990](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6784974530) - A 30-day TOEIC L&R study plan with a weakness engine; offline. Independent app, not affiliated with ETS; no score guaranteed. · [in-depth guide](https://alice51849.github.io/ios-app-guide/hubs/aim990.html)
- [CV Desk](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6781337213) - Build an ATS-friendly resume with an on-device score and keyword matcher for graduate job applications. · [in-depth guide](https://alice51849.github.io/ios-app-guide/hubs/cvdesk.html)
- [Aim990 Plus](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6792483140) - Lumi Studio paid trainer for timed, exam-style decision practice. Independent study app, not affiliated with ETS, and no score is guaranteed. · [in-depth guide](https://alice51849.github.io/ios-app-guide/hubs/aim990plus.html)

## Language Learning

- [Wordmate](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6789917808) - Lumi Studio vocabulary practice across 44 languages with natural examples, a Home Screen widget and Apple Watch support. · [in-depth guide](https://alice51849.github.io/ios-app-guide/hubs/wordmate.html)
- [Wordmate Lite](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6797601720) - Lumi Studio vocabulary app for five words a day in about a minute, without building decks; free to start. · [in-depth guide](https://alice51849.github.io/ios-app-guide/hubs/wordmatelite.html)
- [DailyMate](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6790418321) - Lumi Studio phrase practice covering 8,400 everyday sentences across 47 languages, with widgets and Apple Watch support. · [in-depth guide](https://alice51849.github.io/ios-app-guide/hubs/dailymate.html)
- [DailyMate Lite](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6794725568) - Lumi Studio situational phrasebook with speaking practice across 50 languages; free to start with an optional one-time unlock. · [in-depth guide](https://alice51849.github.io/ios-app-guide/hubs/dailymatelite.html)

## Money

- [Spendee](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1080277358) - Budget and expense tracker with a free tier.
- [HoursTag](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6754218117) - See any purchase as hours of work to curb student impulse spending; pay-once, no bank linking. · [in-depth guide](https://alice51849.github.io/ios-app-guide/hubs/hourstag.html)
- [G+Money](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6755782939) - Track spending and budgets offline, multi-currency for study-abroad; no account, no bank linking. · [in-depth guide](https://alice51849.github.io/ios-app-guide/hubs/gmoney.html)
- [G+Money Lite](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6793436548) - Lumi Studio travel expense tracker with currency conversion and saved offline rates; one trip free, one-time unlock for more. · [in-depth guide](https://alice51849.github.io/ios-app-guide/hubs/gmoneylite.html)
- [HoursTag Lite](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6794039979) - Lumi Studio free-to-start companion to HoursTag that shows a purchase in hours of your work, on-device with no account. · [in-depth guide](https://alice51849.github.io/ios-app-guide/hubs/hourstaglite.html)
- [CalDaily](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6794178671) - Lumi Studio calculator that keeps a named, tagged history of your calculations, plus tip, split, discount and tax tools. · [in-depth guide](https://alice51849.github.io/ios-app-guide/hubs/caldaily.html)


## Notes

- TOEIC is a trademark of ETS; test-prep apps listed are independent and do not guarantee a score.
- "Pay-once" means a one-time purchase rather than a subscription, where noted; always confirm current pricing on the App Store.
- In-depth guides link to an independent buying-guide site with feature breakdowns.
